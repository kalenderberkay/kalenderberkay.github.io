Pipeline · 04

3D direction meets hand-drawn soul.

Pure text-to-video drifts because there is no spatial anchor. Locking cameras in 3D or character turnarounds in 2D first keeps every cut intentional.

01

Psychological Storyboard

Viewers drop off within 3 to 5 seconds when tension stalls. Every piece starts with a timed three-act beat sheet and animatic before rendering begins.

02

3D Camera or 2D Sheet Lock

For 3D scenes, lens choreography and lighting are blocked in UE5 or Blender. For 2D worlds, hand-drawn keyframes and turnaround sheets lock proportions.

03

Native 3D or Hybrid Motion

Track A renders 100% natively in real-time 3D or hand-drawn 2D with zero AI pixels. Track B passes locked frames through ComfyUI I2I and LTX 2.5.

04

Multi-Format Cinema Master

Editorial 2-to-4 second cuts, sound design, voiceover sync, and compositing delivered in 16:9 widescreen alongside 9:16 vertical cuts for social launch.

FAQ

Process, AI rules & rights.

How visual consistency, platform disclosures, source assets, and milestone approvals work.

You choose the exact track upfront. Under Valve's January 16, 2026 Steam policy, promotional videos containing AI-generated frames require disclosure, whereas pure in-engine or hand-drawn renders do not. Select Track A (100% Native UE5 / Hand-Drawn 2D) when you require zero generative AI pixels. Select Track B (Hybrid 2D/3D + ComfyUI and LTX 2.5) for webcomic teasers, social hooks, or stylized trailers where speed and painterly scale take priority.

For 3D scenes, cameras and lighting are blocked inside UE5 or Blender first, locked start/end viewport stills are stylized via ComfyUI Image-to-Image, and controlled 2-to-4 second motion passes run through LTX 2.5. For 2D webcomics and stories, every cut begins from hand-drawn keyframes and a locked character turnaround sheet.

For webcomics or animated shorts: existing comic panels, character sheets, an audio track, or a 1-page story synopsis. For in-engine 3D cutscenes: your environment/character assets or a build for HUD-free capture. Once I review your brief, I reply within 24 hours with a fixed scope, timeline, and custom quote.

Every commission runs on two structured approval gates: Gate 1 (three-act storyboard animatic and style-frame lock) and Gate 2 (final rendered cut and sound mix). Projects can be funded via 50/50 milestone invoice or protected Upwork Escrow, and full commercial usage rights transfer on completion.

About

Psychology graduate turned world builder.

I'm Berkay Kalender, a 2D and 3D animator, cutscene director, and game designer based in Vilnius, Lithuania.

With a BA in Psychology (Honors) from Istanbul Bilgi University and Multimedia Design studies at VILNIUS TECH, I approach animation from two angles: what makes an audience connect emotionally, and how to hold visual consistency from rough sketch to final cut.

My projects span colorful hand-drawn 2D worlds and real-time 3D scenes: Fancy Stars (original 2D fantasy webcomic series and hybrid animated shorts), LETHE (3-minute animated short film), The Hatman (UE5 Sequencer and MetaHuman cutscene), CHLORINE (UE5 co-op game), WORN (built solo in 48 hours at Global Game Jam Lithuania 2026), and The Catio (hand-drawn 2D platformer created during Google Game & App Academy).

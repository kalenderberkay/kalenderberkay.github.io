Process

Locked cameras and character sheets. Consistent frames.

Prompt-only AI video loses faces and room geometry between cuts. Blocking cameras in 3D or drawing 2D keyframes first keeps characters on-model from shot to shot.

01

Script & Timed Animatic

Every project starts with a shot list and rough timed animatic so pacing, camera angles, and cut lengths are approved before rendering.

02

3D Camera or 2D Keyframes

For 3D scenes, cameras and lighting are set in UE5 or Blender. For 2D projects, hand-drawn keyframes and character sheets lock proportions.

03

In-Engine or AI-Assisted Render

Track A renders directly in Unreal Engine 5 or hand-drawn 2D with zero AI frames. Track B animates locked frames through ComfyUI and LTX 2.5.

04

Edit, Sound & Delivery

Color grading, sound design, and voice sync delivered in 16:9 widescreen alongside 9:16 vertical cuts for social platforms.

FAQ

Process, AI rules & rights.

How character consistency, Steam disclosures, source files, and milestone approvals work.

You pick the track upfront. Under Valve's Steam policy, store videos containing AI-generated frames require disclosure, whereas pure in-engine or hand-drawn renders do not. Choose Track A (100% Native UE5 / Hand-Drawn 2D) when you need zero AI frames. Choose Track B (2D/3D + ComfyUI and LTX 2.5) for webcomic teasers, social clips, or stylized trailers where faster turnaround fits best.

For 3D scenes, cameras and lighting are blocked in UE5 or Blender first, start and end frames are styled in ComfyUI Image-to-Image, and short 2-to-4 second motion passes run through LTX 2.5. For 2D projects, every shot starts from hand-drawn keyframes and a locked character turnaround sheet.

For webcomics or animated shorts: existing comic panels, character art, an audio track, or a short summary. For 3D game cutscenes: your environment and character assets or a build for HUD-free capture. After reviewing your brief, I reply within 24 hours with a fixed scope, schedule, and quote.

Every project runs on two approval checkpoints: Checkpoint 1 (timed storyboard animatic and style frame) and Checkpoint 2 (final rendered cut and sound mix). Payment runs 50/50 by invoice or through Upwork Escrow, and full commercial rights transfer on delivery.

About

Trained in psychology, working in 2D and 3D.

I'm Berkay Kalender, a 2D and 3D animator, cutscene director, and game designer based in Vilnius, Lithuania.

With a BA in Psychology (Honors) from Istanbul Bilgi University and Multimedia Design studies at VILNIUS TECH, I approach animation from two angles: what makes an audience connect emotionally, and how to hold visual consistency from rough sketch to final cut.

Recent work includes Fancy Stars (an original 2D fantasy comic and animated series), LETHE (a 3-minute animated short film), The Hatman (an Unreal Engine 5 Sequencer and MetaHuman cutscene), CHLORINE (a UE5 co-op game), WORN (made solo in 48 hours at Global Game Jam Lithuania 2026), and The Catio (a 2D platformer built during Google Game & App Academy).
